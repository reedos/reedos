High-speed and RF hardware engineering during the week. Wildlife photography on
the weekends.

Both ends of that turn into software eventually — something needs measuring, or
simulating, or sorting, and nothing off the shelf quite fits. What I build tends
to be local-first and single-purpose: it runs on your own machine and does one
job properly.

**[Field Catalog](https://github.com/reedos/field-catalog-worker)** is the one
that is public so far — a culling and cataloguing app for photographers who come
home with more frames than time. Compare a burst frame by frame, keep the one
that works, let the rest go. Nothing leaves your machine unless you ask it to.
